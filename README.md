EDA SQL Project

In this Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) SQL Project), I analyze the Gold Layer of my previously developed Data Warehouse to extract meaningful business insights from clean, structured, and reporting-ready data.

I begin with Database Exploration, where I inspect available tables and columns using INFORMATION_SCHEMA to understand the database structure. Next, I perform Dimension Exploration to identify unique countries, product categories, subcategories, and products.

I then conduct Date Exploration to determine the available sales period, earliest and latest orders, overall time span, and customer age range. In Measure Exploration, I calculate key business metrics such as total sales, total quantity sold, average selling price, total orders, products, customers, and active customers.

Through Magnitude Analysis, I compare business performance across countries, genders, product categories, and customers, including revenue and quantity distribution. Finally, I perform Ranking Analysis using SQL window functions such as DENSE_RANK() to identify the top 5 highest-revenue products and the 5 worst-performing products.

Overall, this project demonstrates my ability to use SQL for business-oriented EDA, KPI calculation, segmentation, aggregation, joins, and ranking analysis to transform warehouse data into actionable insights.
